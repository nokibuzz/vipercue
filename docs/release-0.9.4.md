## Facilitator microphone in Feedbacker

Feedbacker can now capture two audio sources at once in a Meet or Teams workshop. The meeting audio follows the selected Candidate or Reviewer label. The facilitator's own microphone is transcribed separately and labeled as a question or follow-up, even while Candidate is selected. The ASMA recap receives the questions and answers in their spoken order, so its feedback can address what the facilitator actually asked. Local history keeps the same labeled turns.

To use it, choose **Feedbacker → System audio · Meet / Teams**, leave **Capture my microphone as facilitator questions** checked, and grant both audio permissions. Headphones help prevent the meeting audio from leaking into the facilitator microphone. For a single room microphone, turn off the separate capture and label speakers with the Question/Candidate/Reviewer buttons as before.

This is source-based attribution, not speaker recognition within the meeting audio. Intel (`x64`) and Apple Silicon (`arm64`) DMGs remain ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. See the [macOS installation guide](https://github.com/nokibuzz/vipercue/blob/main/docs/macos-install.md) for the standard Gatekeeper warning.
